# **Soldering Trainings**

The AMP Lab provides three levels of soldering training for students:

<a class="btn" href="/soldering/basic">Basic Soldering Training</a>
<a class="btn" href="/soldering/intermediate">Intermediate Soldering Training</a>
<a class="btn" href="/soldering/advanced">Advanced Soldering Training</a>

Completion of Basic Soldering Training or higher is required to use soldering equipment unsupervised in the AMP Lab. Anyone caught using soldering equipment without the required training may be denied access to the AMP Lab for at least one semester.

## Soldering Training Signup

Basic and Intermediate Soldering Training sessions can be scheduled through the AMP Lab booking page. Basic Soldering Training must be completed before beginning Intermediate Soldering Training.

<a class="button is-link" href="/soldering/booking">

    Soldering Training Sign Up
</a>

## Advanced Soldering Training

Advanced Soldering Training is scheduled separately. Intermediate Soldering Training must be completed before beginning Advanced Soldering Training.

Please email the Soldering Training Team to schedule an Advanced Soldering Training session:

<a class="button is-link" href="mailto:AMPLabSolderTrainingTeam@groups.office365.vt.edu">

    AMPLabSolderTrainingTeam@groups.office365.vt.edu
</a>

## Important Notes

**Note: Instructors are student volunteers. If you haven't received a reply, please send a follow-up email.**

**If no soldering training sessions are shown on the booking website, this means that all currently available sessions are full. Additional sessions will be added as instructor availability permits.**

**If you urgently need soldering training and cannot wait for another session to become available, please email shanew05@vt.edu and aleks@vt.edu. Urgent training requests will be evaluated on a case-by-case basis and are not guaranteed.**
