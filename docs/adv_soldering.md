
# **Advanced Soldering Training**

Advanced soldering training builds upon the skills learned in intermediate soldering training and focuses on more complex surface-mount soldering techniques.

Upon successfully completing the training, you'll gain experience soldering small surface-mount components and operating equipment such as stencil printers and reflow ovens.

Students who complete advanced soldering training are strongly encouraged to become soldering trainers at AMP Lab.

**Objective:** \
After completing advanced soldering training, you will be able to confidently solder the following surface-mount components:

- SOIC-8, 1.27 mm pitch
- TSSOP-8, 0.65 mm pitch
- VSSOP-8, 0.5 mm pitch
- SOT-23-5, 0.95 mm pitch
- QFP-44-10x10, 0.8 mm pitch
- QFP-100-12x12, 0.4 mm pitch
- QFN-20-5x5, 0.65 mm pitch
- QFN-20-4x4, 0.5 mm pitch
- QFN-20-3x3, 0.4 mm pitch
- 1206 resistors (3216 metric), 3.2 x 1.6 mm
- 0805 resistors (2012 metric), 2.0 x 1.25 mm
- 0603 resistors (1608 metric), 1.6 x 0.8 mm
- 0402 resistors (1005 metric), 1.0 x 0.5 mm

**Prerequisite:** \
Intermediate Soldering Training at AMP Lab

**Estimated Time:** \
4 Sessions (1 hour each), with additional individual training between sessions

**Practice Board:** \
Do Nothing Board 2

**Certification Board Available:** \
AMP Lab Microcontroller

**Procedure:** \
    ->The instructor will meet with you once a week for four sessions. \
    ->Between sessions, you will practice the techniques covered by the instructor on your own. \
    ->You will first hand solder as much of the practice board as possible. \
    ->You will then use the reflow oven to solder the practice board. \
    ->Once the instructor is satisfied with your progress, you will receive the certification board. \
    ->Solder the certification board to complete the training.
