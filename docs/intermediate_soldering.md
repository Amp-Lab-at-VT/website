
# **Intermediate Soldering Training**

Intermediate soldering training builds upon the foundation of basic soldering techniques and focuses on practical skills for soldering a functional PCB.

Upon successfully completing the training, you'll gain intermediate soldering skills and get to keep the PCB you've assembled during the training!

This training will not cover advanced soldering techniques (techniques for building circuits such as microcontroller boards, PCs, etc.). It is highly recommended that you sign up for advanced soldering training after completing intermediate training.

**Objective:** \
After completing intermediate soldering training, you will be able to:
- Confidently populate a PCB with a variety of surface-mount (SMD) and through-hole (THT) components.
- Identify and correct common soldering mistakes.
- Operate a soldering iron with different tips.
- Use tools such as microscope cameras for precision soldering.

**Prerequisite:** \
Basic Soldering Training at AMP Lab

**Estimated Time:** \
1 Session (around 3 hours)

**Practice Board:** \
Do Nothing Board 1

**Certification Board Available:** \
B-BOP: Bipolar Breadboard Power Supply

**Procedure:** \
    ->The instructor will go over the safety and lab rules. \
    ->The instructor will teach you how to solder surface-mount components on the practice board. \
    ->Solder a certification board of your choice.
